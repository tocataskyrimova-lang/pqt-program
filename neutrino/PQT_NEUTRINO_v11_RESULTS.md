## 3. Analysis

The introduction of direct Gen1↔Gen3 coupling ($\zeta$) in version v11 successfully enabled **CP-violation** ($J_{CP} \approx 0.02$) and allowed for a slightly better fit of the mass ratio and $\theta_{12}$. However, the **$\theta_{23}$ trade-off remains unresolved**. The sensitivity of $\theta_{23}$ to the $\zeta$ perturbation is extremely low in the current hexagonal coupling framework. Increasing $\zeta$ to the magnitude required to rotate $\theta_{23}$ by 10 degrees (to reach 49°) leads to a loss of positive-definiteness in the mass matrix (negative eigenvalues), rendering the solution unphysical.

**Conclusion**: PQT Neutrino v11 is a necessary structural evolution (Hermitian complex space), but it is **not the final breakthrough** for the PMNS precision fit. The $\theta_{23}$ stagnation at ~59° suggests that either the generation partition or the $\sigma$-charge distribution requires further fundamental adjustment beyond simple Gen1-Gen3 cross-coupling.
